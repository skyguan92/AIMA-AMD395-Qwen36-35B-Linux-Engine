# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically rigorous and harassment-free
community. Be constructive, assume good faith, distinguish evidence from
opinion, and focus criticism on code and claims rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate exposure of private information, or sustained disruption
of project spaces. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will
be reviewed promptly and handled with appropriate confidentiality.
